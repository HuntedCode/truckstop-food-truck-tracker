# Curbfeast — Logo & Brand Identity Brief

> A self-contained brief to hand to a designer (Fiverr or otherwise). Created 2026-06-05.
> Everything below is copy-paste ready. The "Colors" and "Typography" values are fixed; the mark concept is a direction, not a cage.

## About the brand

**Curbfeast** is a mobile and web app that helps people find local **food trucks**: where they are, and when. It serves two audiences:

- **Customers** discover trucks near them, follow their favorites, and get notified when a truck they love is nearby or live.
- **Truck owners** post where and when they'll be, and promote their business.

The heart of the product is **location + time**: "which trucks are near me, right now or soon." The brand should radiate the small joy of tracking down a great street-food meal.

## The feeling we want

**Appetizing, local, lively, and trustworthy.** Warm and energetic. It celebrates small, independent street-food businesses. Friendly and full of character, but with **restraint**: clean, clear, and confident, never cutesy, greasy, or corporate.

The identity has to flex across **two tempos**:

- **Customer side:** warm, bold, a little playful, photo-forward.
- **Owner side:** the same brand, but calmer and more professional (a tool to run a business).

The logo must feel at home in **both**, on a bright tomato-red header and on a clean cream/white one.

## Name & wordmark

- The name is **Curbfeast**: one word, single capital C. **Not** "CurbFeast" and **not** "Curb Feast."
- It's a coined blend of **curb** (curbside, where trucks serve) + **feast** (appetite, abundance).
- You may visually distinguish the two halves in the wordmark (for example "Curb" and "feast" in two colors or two weights), but it is always written and read as one word.

## Mark / icon concept (a strong direction, surprise us within it)

The product's spine is **location + time**, so the leading idea is a **map pin fused with food**:

- A **map pin** whose body suggests food: a plate, a fork, rising steam, a bun, a taco, or a small **food-truck** silhouette.
- Because the name is "feast," lean **appetizing** (steam, fork, plate) rather than a purely literal truck.
- Bonus, not required: a subtle hint of a **curb** line as a baseline or ground.

We want a clean, rounded, friendly symbol that instantly reads as **"food + here."** It must work **tiny** (an app icon at 48px) and in **a single flat color**. Surprise us with a smart concept, but keep it simple and scalable.

## Deliverables requested

1. **Primary logo** — mark + "Curbfeast" wordmark in a horizontal lockup.
2. **Stacked lockup** — mark above the wordmark, for square-ish spaces.
3. **Standalone mark / app icon** — the symbol alone, square, crisp at 48px (doubles as app icon, social avatar, favicon).
4. **Wordmark-only** version (no mark).
5. **Color variants** of each: full-color, all-white (reversed, for the tomato header), and single-color espresso/black (mono).
6. **Usage basics** — clear-space and minimum-size guidance (a short note or one-page mini sheet).
7. **Source + exports** — layered vector source (**SVG** plus AI or Figma), transparent **PNG** at multiple sizes, and a **favicon / app-icon** set.

## Colors (use these exact values)

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| **Primary — tomato red** (the brand's main color) | `#E84A27` |
| Primary dark (pressed/hover) | `#C73C1E` |
| **Accent — mustard amber** (secondary) | `#F6A623` |
| Accent dark | `#D98911` |
| Background — warm cream | `#FFF8F0` |
| Surface — white | `#FFFFFF` |
| Ink — espresso (dark text / mono mark) | `#2B2118` |

The mark should be recognizable primarily in **tomato `#E84A27`**, must also work in **solid white** (placed on a tomato background) and in **solid espresso `#2B2118`** (single-color/mono). Amber is a supporting accent, not the lead.

## Typography

- **Brand / display font: Poppins** (weights 600/700), a friendly geometric sans. Build or harmonize the wordmark with Poppins. **Custom lettering is welcome** if it stays in that friendly-geometric spirit.
- The product's body font is Inter (FYI only, not needed for the logo).

## Do's and don'ts

**Do**
- Rounded, friendly, confident. Flat or only lightly dimensional. Modern and clean.
- Make sure it survives at 48px and in one flat color.
- Keep it warm and appetizing.

**Don't**
- No clip-art trucks, no photorealism, no gradients or drop shadows used as a crutch.
- Not childish/cutesy, not corporate/sterile, not a generic "delivery app" look.
- Avoid tired fast-food clichés (golden-arches energy, grease, cartoon mascots).
- Never spell it "CurbFeast" or "Curb Feast."

## References / mood (for vibe only, do not copy)

Warm street-food and farmers-market energy, neighborly and local, with the friendly clarity of a good maps app. Target tone: **appetizing + neighborly + a touch playful.**
