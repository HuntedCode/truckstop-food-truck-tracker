# Chuckwagon — Logo & Brand Identity Brief

> A self-contained brief to hand to a designer (Fiverr or otherwise). Updated 2026-06-06.
> Copy-paste ready. The "Colors" values are fixed; the mark and type are a direction to elevate, not a cage.

## About the brand

**Chuckwagon** is a mobile and web app that helps people find local **food trucks**: where they are, and when. Two audiences:

- **Customers** discover trucks near them, follow favorites, and get notified when a truck they love is nearby or live.
- **Truck owners** post where and when they'll be, and promote their business.

The heart of the product is **location + time**: "which trucks are near me, right now or soon."

**The name is the brand's gift:** a *chuckwagon* is the 1866 horse-drawn mobile field kitchen, **America's original food truck**, the wagon that rolled out to feed people wherever they gathered. Lean into that heritage.

## The feeling we want

**Warm, characterful vintage-Americana, appetizing and welcoming.** Think frontier provisions, hand-painted signage, the wagon rolling in to feed the crowd, hearty and hospitable.

**Two important guardrails:**
1. **Heritage, not kitsch.** Evoke vintage/frontier warmth, not literal cowboy costume (no lassos, sheriff badges, saloon-poster clichés, cartoon mascots).
2. **Cuisine-neutral.** Food trucks serve tacos, coffee, Korean BBQ, vegan, boba, everything. The vibe must say "characterful local food," not "American BBQ/steakhouse." The heritage is in the *wagon/journey/hospitality* story, not the menu.

It also flexes across **two tempos**: customer side warm/bold/photo-forward; owner side the same brand but calmer and more businesslike. The mark must work on a bright tomato header and a clean cream one.

## Mark / icon concept (the direction)

The name hands you a distinctive, ownable symbol: **a stylized chuckwagon / covered wagon**, the original food truck. Refine and modernize it; don't make it a literal historical illustration.

- A clean, friendly **covered-wagon silhouette**, or its most iconic part, a **wagon wheel**, as the simplest app-icon form.
- **Bonus, not required:** fuse it with a subtle **map pin** (the wagon sitting in/on a pin), to nod to the "find it near you" spine.
- It must read instantly as "a little food wagon, here," work at **48px** (app icon) and in **one flat color**.

This replaces the earlier generic "map-pin + food" idea, the wagon is more distinctive and on-name. Surprise us within it.

## Name & wordmark

- The name is **Chuckwagon**: one word, single capital C.
- A custom, slightly **vintage/hand-lettered wordmark** is very welcome (see Typography). It can carry the most "twang"; keep it legible.

## Typography

- **Brand / display (wordmark, headlines):** lean **vintage-Americana**, a characterful **slab serif** or tasteful western-tinged display / hand-lettered feel. This is where the personality lives.
- **UI / body:** stays a clean, highly legible sans (we currently use **Inter**, with **Poppins** for some headings) so the app is fast to read, the twang seasons brand moments, it never compromises clarity. Deliver the wordmark as art; we don't need a full UI typeface.

## Colors (use these exact values)

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| **Primary — tomato red** | `#E84A27` |
| Primary dark (pressed/hover) | `#C73C1E` |
| **Accent — mustard amber** | `#F6A623` |
| Accent dark | `#D98911` |
| Background — warm cream | `#FFF8F0` |
| Surface — white | `#FFFFFF` |
| Ink — espresso (dark text / mono mark) | `#2B2118` |

This warm tomato/mustard/cream/espresso set already reads vintage-Americana, so it fits the heritage direction well. The mark should be recognizable primarily in **tomato `#E84A27`**, and also work in **solid white** (on tomato) and **solid espresso** (mono). (An earthy wagon-wood brown may get added in a later design pass; the above are the current source of truth.)

## Deliverables requested

1. **Primary logo** — wagon mark + "Chuckwagon" wordmark, horizontal lockup.
2. **Stacked lockup** — mark above wordmark.
3. **Standalone mark / app icon** — the symbol alone, square, crisp at 48px (app icon, social avatar, favicon).
4. **Wordmark-only** version.
5. **Color variants** of each: full-color, all-white (reversed), single-color espresso (mono).
6. **Usage basics** — clear-space + minimum-size guidance (short sheet).
7. **Source + exports** — layered vector (**SVG** + AI or Figma), transparent **PNG** at multiple sizes, favicon/app-icon set.

## Do's and don'ts

**Do:** warm, characterful, confident; flat or lightly dimensional; survives at 48px and in one flat color; appetizing.
**Don't:** literal cowboy kitsch (lassos/badges/saloon posters), cartoon mascots, photorealism, gradients/drop-shadows as a crutch, generic "delivery app" look, or anything that pins the brand to one cuisine.
